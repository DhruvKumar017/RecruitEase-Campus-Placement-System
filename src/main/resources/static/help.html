Frequently Asked Questions

Open the student dashboard, go to Eligible Companies or Placement Drives, select a company and click the Apply button. Your application status will appear in the Applications section.
Open your student profile page, click Edit Profile, update your personal, academic or skill information and save the changes.
When the admin schedules an aptitude test, eligible students receive access according to the test date and time. Submit answers before the timer ends.
Your application status will change to Shortlisted, Selected or Rejected. You can check this from the Applications section of your dashboard.
Contact the placement administrator or use the password reset option when it is enabled on the login page.
Contact the Placement Cell through the phone number or email shown in the Contact Placement Cell card on this page.
