ABOUT RECRUITEASE

One Platform for Every Placement Activity

Students can register, update profiles, apply to companies, attempt aptitude tests, request interviews and view final placement status. Admin can manage companies, drives, applications, tests, interviews and placements.

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Have Questions?

Important RecruitEase placement process information.

How does a student register on RecruitEase?

Click Student Registration, fill all details, accept terms and create your account.

How is eligibility checked?

Eligibility is calculated using company minimum CGPA and required skills.

How does a student attempt aptitude test?

Students can start only assigned tests and only during the admin scheduled time.

How can a student apply for interview?

After qualifying aptitude test, Apply for Interview option becomes available.

How do students join online interview?

Admin adds Google Meet or Classroom link and student gets Join button in Interviews page.

How is final placement status shown?

Admin can select or directly place a student and status appears in student portal.
